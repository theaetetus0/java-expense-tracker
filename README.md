# Expense Tracker - CPSC 210 Personal Project

## Project Proposal

**What will the application do?**

The application is an expense tracker that tracks a user's monthly income and expenses.
A user will be able to keep tabs on their spendings, manage bills and budgets, as well as get personal insight.
The user will be able to enter their income and add invidual expenses to different types of expense categories.
These categories for expenses include:
- Rent/Mortgage
- Utilities/Telephone
- Groceries
- Insurance
- Health
- Savings/Investments
- Subscriptions
- Leisure
- Others

The application will be able to visualize the user's income and expenses into a graph.
Budgets based on popular budget saving strategies will be recommended to the user.

**Who will use it?**

Individuals who need to keep tabs on their income and purchase history so that they can budget appropriately.
These users may want to see personal analytics and visualize their expenditure throughout the month.
Even if someone may be great at account keeping and money management, 
it is almost always difficult--or at the very least time consuming--to keep track of transactions by hand.
An application that simplifies this process would be quite usefull.

**Why is this project of interest to you?**

I am in the process of learning how to properly budget my money every month and certainly,
it can be quite daunting to encapsulate all your information into comprehensible chunks at once.
I thought that creating an application to simplify the budgeting and finance management process for me
would not only be interting, but also rather usefull.
